## Why I am building this

Little Witch Post explores email literacy through a gentle game loop where kids read, write, and send letters inside a cozy world.

## Core idea

Players help a young witch deliver thoughtful messages, solve small communication puzzles, and unlock new story episodes.

## Current status

The current work is focused on episode structure, progress systems, and the shape of the first playable loop.

## Next steps

- Define the first episode flow.
- Prototype the letter writing interaction.
- Sketch the reward loop for returning players.
